The one question

On the iPad, should the note's text run the full width of the pane like Apple Notes (about 900 pt in landscape), or stay capped at Skrift's 640 pt reading width, which leaves about 350 pt empty on the right? Flip "Text width" to see both.

The Apple Notes frames are redrawn in HTML from Tuur's own iPad screenshots (IMG_5231 to IMG_5238, 2752 x 2064 px, 13 Oct 2026; scale 2752 / 1376 = 2 px per point, confirmed by the 44 pt glass buttons at 88 px and the 340 pt sidebar at 680 px). No screenshot or Apple icon is embedded, the icons are generic strokes, and the note text, folder names and tags are invented to the same line lengths. The Skrift frames follow Shared/UI/NoteCardView.swift (row type 16 / 14 / 12.5, the grey Unrated marker, Portfolio-only line 3, 52 pt thumbnail), SkriftMobile/Features/MemosList/MemosListView.swift (375 pt list column, the pinned list toggle), MemosListView+Header.swift (verb row, Process, chips), Root/AppTabView.swift (Notes, Library, Review, Settings as a top strip), MemoDetail/MemoDetailView.swift (workbench band, 640 pt reading width, docked player), MemoPageView.swift (Take Photo / Choose from Library), NoteBodyView.swift (square checkboxes, "New person…" in the text menu) and the signed mocks Q349, Q350 and ipad-note-chrome-belongs.
