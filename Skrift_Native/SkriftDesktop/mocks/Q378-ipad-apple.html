The one question

On the iPad, should the note's text run the full width of the pane like Apple Notes (about 900 pt in landscape), or stay capped at Skrift's 640 pt reading width, which leaves about 350 pt empty on the right? Flip "Text width" to see both.

The Apple Notes frame is redrawn in HTML from Tuur's own iPad screenshot (ipad-list-note.webp, 2000 x 1500 px, 13 Oct 2026). Its scale is 2000 / 1376 = 1.4535 px per point, confirmed by the 44 pt glass buttons (64 px) and the 340 pt sidebar (494 px). No screenshot or Apple icon is embedded, the icons are generic strokes, and the note text is invented to the same line lengths.
