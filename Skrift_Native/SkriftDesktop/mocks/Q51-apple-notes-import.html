QUESTION 1 OF 1

When you import 400 Apple Notes at once, should they land quiet (unrated: nothing polished, nothing exported to Obsidian until you rate one), or rated (the Mac polishes all of them and exports them to your vault)?

Today a Mac import is rated at the 0.1 floor ("an import is consent"), so a 400-note import would queue 400 polishes. The mock picks quiet (PICK B, step 2). Tap its orange flag to see the other answer.

Today, from the source

  • Mac only: Import → pick a folder of Apple Notes .md exports; each file becomes one note.
  • Title = the first # heading, else the filename.
  • Body = the markdown as written, marked typed, glyph "Apple Note", never re-transcribed.
  • Pictures in Attachments/ are copied and relinked; HEIC becomes JPEG.
  • Phone: one shared or opened .md becomes the body text of a note.
  • Date: every note is dated to the import moment. D18 says it must be the note's creation date.
  • Subfolders are skipped, so a nested Notes folder never imports.
  • Every note copies the whole Attachments/ folder, not only its own pictures.
  • Phone: pictures are not imported; the (Attachments/…) links stay dangling.
  • Checklists, tags, folders: no mapping step; whatever text the export holds passes through.
  • No report: a file Skrift can't read is skipped silently.
