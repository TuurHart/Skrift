Phone and Mac. Each state shows Skrift on one side and a reference panel of Apple Notes on the other, redrawn by hand in HTML from Tuur’s own iOS 27 screenshots of a throwaway folder (phone) and his macOS screenshots (Mac). No screenshot is embedded, there is no Apple branding, and the note text in the reference is invented. Tap things: tabs, play, the pill, the destination chip, Aa, the checklist boxes, the photo (press and hold, then drag).

Picked for you. Overrule any of these.

  1. 1While typing, undo/redo and Done move to the top bar, where Apple Notes has them. The keyboard bar shrinks to Aa · checklist · photo · note link. Find moves into the ⋯ menu. Apple’s table and markup-pen buttons are left out (no tables, C116; markup opens from the photo).
  2. 2The Aa panel holds only D115’s set: Title, Heading, Body · B I U S · bulleted, numbered, checklist. No Subheading, Monostyled, colour, indent or block quote. Each button shows the Markdown it writes as a small caption. Buttons are 40 pt tall now (were 58), close to Apple’s Format rows (D195).
  3. 3Underline writes <u>…</u>, because Markdown has no underline. Obsidian renders it; in Skrift the tags show dim like every other mark.
  4. 4The title is no longer pinned and grows from 17 to 22 pt; it scrolls with the note. The pin was for swiping between notes, which is off (Q314).
  5. 5The Mac gets one Aa button in its band (Apple Notes has one there), plus ⌘B ⌘I ⌘U ⇧⌘X and a Format section in the right-click menu. No floating selection bar on the Mac.
  6. 7The header is three rows, as close to Apple’s calm as Skrift’s fields allow (D195): one grey line (time · place · weather · length, no chip boxes), the title, then rating · Personal · tags on one row. The summary loses its tinted card and becomes a grey line with a hairline under it. On the Mac the two switches sit at the right end of that same row.
  7. 8“New person…” sits right after B I U S in the selection bar (D184), tinted so it reads as Skrift’s own. To keep the bar one row at 402 pt, Paste moves behind ›. On the Mac it is the last entry of the Format block in the right-click menu.
  8. 6Inside a book quote the Aa styles and lists are greyed, so the quote stays a > quote and polish keeps protecting it. B I U S still work there.
