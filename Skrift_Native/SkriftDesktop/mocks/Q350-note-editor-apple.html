The open note and its editing toolbar, next to Apple Notes

Phone and Mac. Each state shows Skrift on one side and a hand-drawn reference panel of Apple Notes on the other. The reference is my own approximate drawing of the public app, never your notes, and carries no Apple branding. Tap things: tabs, play, the pill, the destination chip, Aa, the checklist boxes, the photo (press and hold, then drag).

1. Where does the note’s title live?
Apple Notes has no title field: the first line of the text is the title. Skrift keeps a separate title (synced on its own, with the suggested-title choice). Both versions are live in the frames below; pick one and the frames follow.

Picked for you. Overrule any of these.

  1. 1While typing, undo/redo and Done move to the top bar, where Apple Notes has them. The keyboard bar shrinks to Aa · checklist · photo · note link. Find moves into the ⋯ menu. Apple’s table and markup-pen buttons are left out (no tables, C116; markup opens from the photo).
  2. 2The Aa panel holds only D115’s set: Title, Heading, Body · B I U S · bulleted, numbered, checklist. No Subheading, Monostyled, colour, indent or block quote. Each button shows the Markdown it writes.
  3. 3Underline writes <u>…</u>, because Markdown has no underline. Obsidian renders it; in Skrift the tags show dim like every other mark.
  4. 4The title is no longer pinned and grows from 17 to 22 pt; it scrolls with the note. The pin was for swiping between notes, which is off (Q314).
  5. 5The Mac gets one Aa button in its band (Apple Notes has one there), plus ⌘B ⌘I ⌘U ⇧⌘X and a Format section in the right-click menu. No floating selection bar on the Mac.
  6. 6Inside a book quote the Aa styles and lists are greyed, so the quote stays a > quote and polish keeps protecting it. B I U S still work there.
