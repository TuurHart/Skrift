1. Which option do you want, A, B, C, or none of them?
Tap one. This only remembers it on this phone; say it in the sitting.
Why no place: the place is its own chip, and only a phone voice recording ever gets one. Typed notes, imports and captures never ask for a location.
MemoDetailView.metaChips (MemoDetailView.swift:2052) adds the place chip only when metadata.location.placeName is set. Only MetadataService.capture() sets it, called from RecordView (line 160) and MemoSaver.applyMetadata (line 740). A typed note is born by Memo.newTyped (Shared/Model/Memo.swift:398) with metadata {"mediaSource":"typed"} and nothing else. If the note on 172 was a voice recording instead, the GPS fix or reverse-geocode returned nil (permission, indoors, no network), and LocationOneShot drops it silently.
