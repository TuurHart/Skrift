Sync health: the list and the note

Q484 · first pass · C155 · D48 · R47 · row from NoteCardView.swift

What you see when iCloud stops taking your notes: iCloud full, signed out, one note still waiting, and the phone or Mac now on a different iCloud account. Pick a state, then flip between the list and the open note. Notes are fake.

My picks, to overrule
1. A banner that stays on the list, not the bottom capsule The failure sits above the search field until it clears, in the amber shape of the two-versions banner. Today's bottom "Syncing with iCloud…" capsule is transient and fades; a quota problem that fades is the thing C155 is fixing. Flip to see the capsule version on the phone:
2. One amber "Not synced yet" pill, in the slot "2 versions" uses On the Mac it replaces the pipeline word (Ready, Exported) the same way "2 versions" does today. The phone's existing Unsynced chip gets a count and becomes the list of these notes; the banner's Show turns it on. Today nothing in the app writes syncStatus, so that chip filters on a value that never changes.
3. An account switch only explains; it offers no button that moves notes Syncing stops, the banner says so, and What now says how to resume: sign back into the old account. "Sync these notes to this account instead" is drawn dashed and does nothing, because it is the one question below.
The one questionAfter an iCloud account switch, should Skrift offer to start syncing this device's notes into the new account, or only stop and tell you to sign back into the old one?
Rules this mock assumes
  • The list shows a failure within 60 seconds of the first failed upload, and clears it at the next upload that works. No toast, no alert.
  • A note is "not synced yet" when it was made or changed after the last upload that worked. CloudKit gives no per-note status (SkriftMobile/Features/Settings/SettingsView.swift:41-43), so this is Skrift's own record.
  • A single waiting note with a healthy account gets the pill and the note line, never a list banner.
  • Unverified: what SwiftData's CloudKit mirror does to local notes when the account changes. The copy "Nothing here was deleted" must be proven before it ships.
  • Neither app can open the iCloud pane of Settings directly, so the copy names the path instead of offering a button.
