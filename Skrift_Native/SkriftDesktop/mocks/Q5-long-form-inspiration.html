The long-form tab: what to steal

Seven apps that hold books, episodes, articles, PDFs or talks, grouped by how they mix them, one line each on what Skrift should take. Three directions at the bottom, each a clickable phone. Pick one.

These are sketches, not screenshots. The page is one file with no outside images, so each app is drawn as a grey wireframe from memory of its layout. None were checked against the current app versions. Open the real apps next to this page before you pick.

The rule the tab has to hold

From D90 (decided 24 Sep): long-form means a place you come back to. The door decides where a thing lands.

Goes to Notes

  • WhatsApp and other messengers
  • Camera, photo of a page
  • Microphone
  • Screenshot

Goes to the tab

  • Audiobook file
  • Podcast feed or show link
  • ePub, PDF from Files
  • Article link, talk

Two hatches, one action each: move to Library on a note, send to a note on a source. A capture always lands in Notes and links back to its source.

Three directions

Same seven sources in each (example data). The top bar, + button, sort chip, row and tile styling are drawn from the current Books tab source. Anything new is outlined in dashed purple.

Tab name

"Library" is the default because D90's hatch and Q6's toast already say "move to Library" and "Added to Library".

A

The Shelf my lean

From: Bound, Apple Podcasts' Up Next, Reader's type chips.Bets: covers look best, and the iPad shelf grid (C229) already works this way.Risks: articles and PDFs have no cover art, so they get typographic covers that may look fake next to real book art.
B

The List

From: Readwise Reader, and today's compact Books row.Bets: honest about mixed types and cheapest to build. It is the current Books list plus a type line and Reading · Later · Done.Risks: it is the least likely to "look really good".
C

The Front Page

From: Apple News, Snipd's snip cards, a feed's "new episode".Bets: the "almost like a news app" candidate. What you're in the middle of leads, and your captures show under it.Risks: captures are notes, so they would show in two tabs. No type filter; sections replace it.

The one question

Which direction is the tab: A Shelf, B List, or C Front Page?

Inside that: C is the only one that shows your captures on this tab. If you want captures there, C is the answer. If captures belong in Notes only, pick between A and B. My lean is A: covers are the best-looking thing Skrift already has, and the iPad shelf is already a grid.
