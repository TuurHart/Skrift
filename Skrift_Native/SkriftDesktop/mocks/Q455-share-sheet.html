Every share offers the rating (C66), and a messenger share can carry who sent it (C123, with roster autocomplete per D37). The sender field does not exist yet in the code; it shows here on the WhatsApp voice memo and photo. Flip Rating + sender above to compare.

PICK 1 · overrule me

The sheet fills the system sheet and starts at the top

Today the extension paints the whole system sheet near-black (#0e0f16) and parks a small card at the bottom, so most of the screen is a dark void. The calm version starts at the top on the app's grey background, like the share sheet Apple Notes shows, and follows light/dark instead of forcing dark.

PICK 2 · overrule me

Save moves to the top right; the big purple button goes

Close (✕) top left, Save top right, as in every iOS 26 sheet. No Sk monogram, no glow on the button (D208: "we don't need all this glowing"), no purple dots before the small print. The small print becomes a plain grey footer under the card.

PICK 3 · overrule me

The receipt happens in place, not on a new screen

Today Save swaps the sheet for a separate card with a green circle that closes after 0.9 s. Here the Save button turns green "Saved", the fields dim, and the footer says what happened; then the sheet closes after the same 0.9 s. "Already in Skrift" and "can't import" stay full screens because nothing was saved.
