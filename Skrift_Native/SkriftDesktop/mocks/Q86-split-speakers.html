  1. 1A switch in the header, under “Include audio in export”. It shows the state at a glance (split or not) and the same switch turns it off. The … menu version is one tap away in the bar and in the question above.
  2. 2The Mac does not ask “How many speakers?” The phone does (Auto, 2 to 5). The Mac’s diarizer takes no count today (diarizer.diarize(audioURL:)), so it always runs Auto. Adding the count is a second change.
  3. 3Turning it on asks first, because it re-listens and replaces your transcript edits. The Mac needs its own word timings, which the phone never uploads, so a split is a full re-transcribe. Turning it off is Flatten to monologue, which also asks, and which the phone gains (it has no way back today).
