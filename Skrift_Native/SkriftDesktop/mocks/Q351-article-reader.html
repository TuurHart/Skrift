The article reader

The piece the Q6 Library mock left out. Share an article link, it lands on the Library shelf, you read it in reader view, select a line, "Add note", say what you think. The note links back. Links that are not articles stay notes. Phone only, example data, every tap target in the phone responds.

Three picks you might overrule

1The share sheet asks: Article or Note

Picked: sharing a link shows two cards in today's share sheet, Article (to Library) and Note. Article is pre-picked unless the link is a known video, post or shop page. Picking Article hides the thought field and the rating, because a source has neither. Same card idiom as today's "Audiobook / Voice note" chooser.

Instead: no question. Skrift decides and shows the D126 toast "Added to Library · add to a note instead", like a shared PDF.

2Your selection is the quote, as-is

Picked: select text, tap "❝ Add note", and the capture screen shows exactly that selection as the quote, then "Record your thoughts". No "Build your quote" sentence rows: you already chose the words with the selection handles. The highlight stays yellow in the article and tapping it opens its note.

Instead: reuse the audiobook capture's tap-the-lines-around-it builder, so text and audio captures look the same.

3In the vault, the site name is the link

Picked: the attribution exports as — *Title*, [site](URL), with the URL in source: as well. No author, as D138 wrote it. In Skrift the note shows plain "— Local-first software, inkandswitch.com" plus a jump-back chip.

Instead: the bare URL on its own line under the attribution, or [[Author]] first like a book quote.

--- date: 2026-10-13 source: https://www.inkandswitch.com/local-first/ --- *Your data should live on your own device first, and the network should only carry copies.* — *Local-first software*, [inkandswitch.com](https://www.inkandswitch.com/local-first/) This is the CloudKit bet in one line. The phone is the real copy and iCloud is the courier, not the other way round.

The one question

When you share a link, should Skrift ask "Article or Note", or decide by itself and let you switch after?

A shared PDF already goes straight to Library with "add to a note instead" (D126). Links are harder to call: the share sheet cannot load the page, so Skrift only knows the link's address until the app fetches it. The mock asks, with the guess pre-picked, because a wrong silent guess costs you a trip to fix it.

Ask (drawn)Two cards in the share sheet, guess pre-picked. One more glance per share, no surprises.
DecideSame toast as a PDF: "Added to Library · add to a note instead". Zero questions, and the typed thought and rating disappear from article shares.
  • Either way, when the app fetches the page and finds no article text, it saves the link as a note and says so. Try "Links that stay notes", last row.
