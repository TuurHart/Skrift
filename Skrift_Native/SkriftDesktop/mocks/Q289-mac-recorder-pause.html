Three picks you might overrule

A

The discard question is a popover on the ×, not a window alert

It points at the button you pressed and stays inside the sidebar. Keep is the default (Return); Discard is red and never the default. The × sits at the far left of the transport, as far from Stop as the row allows, like the phone's X in its top-left corner.

B

Paused swaps the meter for the word "Paused" and drains the red

The tint goes to the neutral chip fill, the dot goes hollow and stops pulsing, the time freezes in grey. The pause button turns into ▶ Resume in the same spot. The sidebar row and the pane's caret go quiet too. The other way: keep the red and show a frozen flat meter.

C

While the question is open, the take keeps recording

Watch the time in frame 3: it keeps counting. Keep changes nothing, so no words are lost if you hesitate. The other way: opening the question pauses the take, and Keep resumes it.

The question for sign-off

When you press × and the question is up, should the take keep recording (pick C) or pause until you answer?

It decides what Keep means: "nothing happened" or "carry on". Copy and button order follow from it.
