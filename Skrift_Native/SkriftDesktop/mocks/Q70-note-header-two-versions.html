TodayAs built, from source

Phone and iPad run the same noteHeaderCore: chips, tags 8 pt lower, the importance card 14 pt lower, then the orange fading line 8 pt lower. The card is about 100 pt tall because each ball sits in a 44 pt touch target and the sync line sits under a divider with 20 pt of margin. The Mac stacks title, chips, tags and the card 13 pt apart and shows no fading line. A typed note today carries only the date chip.

1B refined: the card becomes one pill

  • Row 1 is one wrapping row: a date + place chip, the weather chip, tags at 26 pt, + tag. The daypart chip (Afternoon) is dropped because it repeats the time.
  • Row 2 always holds the pill, with the orange fading line beside it. In Q50 the pill wandered with the tag count; here it has one fixed home.
  • Tap Not rated once and it is Passing. Once rated, a tap opens the four words in place (Not rated, Passing, Useful, Important); pick one. Important to Passing is 2 taps, where Q50’s cycle took 3.
  • The sync sentence leaves the header. The pill colour carries it: grey outline = left alone, violet = will be processed.
  • Mac: gets the fading line too (new; the Mac header has none today) and the phone date format with the time.
  • 1Phone: the pill and the fading line share one line with about 10 pt to spare. A longer date or larger text size pushes the line under the pill (+17 pt).
  • 2Nothing on screen says what a rating does any more. A new user learns violet = processed by doing it once.

2Today, squeezed: same parts, gaps cut

  • Same parts, same order: chip row, tags at 30 pt, the importance card with Importance, the balls, the tier word and the sync line, then the fading line.
  • Gaps cut: tags 8 to 6 pt under the chips; card 14 to 8 pt under the tags; card top padding 13 to 0; the divider and its 20 pt of margin gone; fading line 8 to 6 pt.
  • The balls keep full 44 pt touch targets. The sync line tucks up 8 pt into the empty bottom of those targets.
  • Mac: the sync line moves onto the balls’ row, since the Mac has the width. Gaps 13 to 8 pt.
  • 1Phone/iPad: the balls’ touch area now overlaps the start of the sync line. A tap on the words “rated — left” can rate the note.
  • 2Still the tallest of the two by far: the 44 pt ball row is the floor, so this cannot go much lower without smaller targets.
Every frame is live and shares one rating: tap a ball, the pill, or a word in the pill’s picker, and all frames follow. Heights are measured from the rendered frame, top of the title row to the first line of the body, and compared against the Today frame for the same device and note kind (Q50 measured the phone voice header at 243 pt). Typed frames in 1 and 2 carry place and weather because typed notes will record them (D151); Today’s typed frame is the app as built, without them. App sizes, colours and copy come from source: MemoPageView.noteHeaderCore, ThreeBallImportanceView with the phone (15 / 44 pt) and Mac (10 / 20 pt) styles in SignificanceCircles.swift, ThreeBallScale names and sync copy, MemoSpine.oneLiner, TagEditorRow, NoteProperties, Palette.swift. The phone nav bar, iPad side panels and Mac window chrome are placeholders. The Mac column width is approximate.
