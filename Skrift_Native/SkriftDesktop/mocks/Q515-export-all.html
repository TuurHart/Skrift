Export all notes as one zip

One new row in Settings on the iPhone and the Mac. Tap it, Skrift builds a zip of every note not in the trash, then the phone opens the share sheet and the Mac asks where to save. Your vault is never touched.

Two picks, my recommendation shown
1
The row sits under iCloud sync on both apps, since it exists for the account-switch case. Other option: its own "Your notes" section above Appearance.
2
Locked notes go in the zip. D218 says every note not in the trash, and a lock hides a note without encrypting it (C213). Other option: leave locked notes out, as vault export does (C161).
