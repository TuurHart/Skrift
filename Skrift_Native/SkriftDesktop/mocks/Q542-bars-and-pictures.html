Two choices in the note editor

Every phone below is live: tap the buttons, tap the note, type on the keys. Your picks go at the bottom. The note and the pictures are made up.

1. Bars while typing

While you type, a note has two bars: one at the top of the screen and one above the keyboard. Could they be one?

My recommendation: one bar at the bottomEverything stays near the thumb and the note gets its top back.

The downsideYou signed off today's arrangement in October because it copies Apple Notes, and Undo moves.

2. Picture sizes

You said small should be a small picture and large a large picture. Today it gives a large picture or a banner you tap to open.

Today, as the app draws it

The proposal

Small becomes a real small picture in the text column. Large fills the text width. I tried small at two sizes, and neither crops anything. Tap a picture to switch it. It stays where it is in the note.

Where the switch lives

TodayHold the picture, tap the arrow, tap View as Large. Three steps. A plain tap opens it full screen.
ProposalTap the picture. One step, in place, and the same while typing. There is no button on the picture: the whole picture is the switch.
BothThe hold menu keeps View as Small and View as Large. The ⋯ menu keeps Set All to Small and Set All to Large.

What the proposal costsA tap can only do one thing. If it switches size, opening a picture full screen (and marking it up) moves into the hold menu. Pictures also lose the "Photo, JPEG, 1.2 MB" line, and you signed the banner on 13 October as Apple's small view. iPad and Mac draw the same banner today, so they would change too unless you say phone only.

A question, not a decisionShould videos and files follow the same idea? Today a small video or file is also this banner. A small video could be a small frame with a play button. A file has no picture to show, so it may have to stay a banner.
