The Library tab

Direction A, the Shelf, as you picked it on the Q5 board. Long-form sources only: book, episode, article, PDF, talk. Your captures stay in Notes and link back. Every tap target in the phone below responds. Example data throughout.

Three picks you might overrule

1Tapping a source opens it straight away

Picked: a tile tap plays or opens the source, same as a book tap today. The ❝ 5 pill on the cover is its own button and opens that source's notes.

Instead: a tile tap opens a source page first (cover, notes, buttons) and you press play there. One more tap every time you just want to listen.

2"Add note" lives inside the source, not on the shelf

Picked: capture keeps today's verb "Add note" (the player, the mini bar and the capture screen all say it). It sits in the player, the mini bar and the PDF/article reader, because a capture needs a spot. "Send to a note" sits in the ⋯ menu and the long-press menu.

Instead: call it "Capture" as D126 wrote it, or put both verbs on every tile.

3The empty tab gets four doors

Picked: one row per source type (audiobook, podcast, ePub or PDF, article), each opening its own door. This drops today's rule of one import button (AudiobookLibraryView.swift keeps a passive hint line only).

Instead: one "Add" button that opens the same list as the +.

The one question

When you "Send to a note" from a source, does the source stay in Library?

This decides whether a source and a note can share one thing, and it sets how "move to Library" works in reverse. The mock does what I lean to: the source stays, and a new note opens in Notes with a card that jumps back to it.

Stays (drawn)A new note holds a link card to the source. The book keeps its place, progress and "N notes" count.
LeavesThe source becomes a note and drops off the shelf, the exact mirror of "move to Library". Progress and the notes count go with it.
  • Same question in reverse: "Move to Library" on a note with a PDF moves the PDF and keeps your words in Notes as a linked note. Try it on "A note with a PDF".
