Where the verb lives

"Fix quote…" sits in the ⋯ sheet right after Split speakers, so it lands in the shared NoteMenuItem order as a "to the recording" verb (after flattenToMonologue, before retranscribe). It only appears on a note whose body opens with a quote block (CaptureQuote.split != nil).

Not picked: long-press the quote block itself. Faster to find, but the quote already takes taps for seek-to-word during playback (Q83).

Pick 1 · one word at a time

You tap the misheard word and retype it. If the engine split one word in two ("or gastric"), "Include next word" grows the selection first. Each fix keeps the audio slot of the words it replaces.

Not picked: free-typing over the whole quote. Then every save needs a word diff to re-find which audio goes with which word, and a slip can shift the karaoke for the rest of the note.

Pick 2 · fix card under the quote

The card opens inline, right under the quote, so the keyboard can't cover the word you are fixing. The ramble dims and is untouchable until Done.

Not picked: a separate sheet (loses the surrounding words), or a bar docked above the keyboard (on an iPhone 13 that leaves about four lines of quote visible).

Pick 3 · no trace after Done

Once you tap Done, the fixed words look like every other word in the quote, on the phone, on the Mac and in the export. The words the engine heard stay in the timings file only, so "Put back" works inside Fix mode later.

Try it: tap "both" → type "boats" → Fix. Then "or" → Include next word → "orgastic". Then "born" → "borne". Done gives an Undo toast.

How a fix stays on its audio

The quote audio never changes. Every word in the quote owns a time slot in the note's timings file. A fix rewrites the words and keeps the slots: one word for one word keeps its slot exactly; "or gastric" → "orgastic" joins two slots into one; one word typed as two splits its slot evenly.
