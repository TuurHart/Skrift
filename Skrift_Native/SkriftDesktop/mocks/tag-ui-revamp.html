The C93 rules, and where each one shows

  • Comma or new line splits. Lisbon, market becomes two chips. Return and comma both add and keep the field open for the next tag.
  • Needs a letter or digit. [] is refused with a line under the row; the field keeps the text so you can fix it.
  • # stripped once, case kept. #design lands as design; Wood stays Wood.
  • Case-variants fold to the first spelling (D23). LISBON on a note that has Lisbon adds nothing; the existing chip pulses and the line says so.
  • All four destination words are tags. inspiration is accepted and says it will ask the archive for a credit (today's copy, kept).
  • Typeahead is prefix, case-insensitive, and shows the spelling you already use. A Create chip leads only when the text is new everywhere.
