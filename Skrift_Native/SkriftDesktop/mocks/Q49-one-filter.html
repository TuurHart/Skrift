One filter mechanism for the notes list

Build 172 feel check: the chips plus the Filter icon read as "difficult or tricky". Below is what ships today, drawn from source, then three ways to make it one thing. Every frame is clickable: chips filter the fake list, the icon or button opens its sheet or popover.

1. Which option do we build: A, B, C, or none? Tap through all four tabs on the phone. Each option shows the phone and the Mac sidebar, and what it costs you. My pick: A. It keeps the counts on the chips (D135) and drops the hidden sheet.

Today: four chips, then a Filter icon that opens a second, different filter

The chips pick a status and show counts. The icon hides sort, a date range, Unsynced, and on the phone a second copy of Unrated. Both apply at once, and the icon only lights up for what is inside it.

Found in source: the phone shows the Unrated chip and a Not rated toggle in the sheet for the same set of notes (MemosListView.swift:338 passes showNotRated: !isRegular). Tap Done, then open the icon and turn on Not rated: the list goes empty and nothing on the bar says why.

A · The chips carry everything

No icon. The chip row gets two more chips, Date and Unsynced, after the four status chips. Sort becomes a word at the end of the row (Newest ↓) that steps to the next order on each tap, the idiom the code already has as MemoSort.next and SidebarSort.next. Date opens a small strip under the row, not a sheet. The duplicate Not rated toggle is gone.

What you give up: on a 390 pt phone the row no longer fits. Date and Unsynced sit past the right edge and you swipe the row to reach them. The row also mixes two kinds of chip: the four status chips pick one, the dashed ones stack on top.

B · One Filter button, the chips move inside it

The chip row is replaced by one button that names what you are looking at: All notes · Newest, or Needs Work 3 · Oldest · from 22 Sep. Tapping it opens one sheet (phone) or popover (Mac) with Show (the four statuses, each with its count), Sort, Date and Unsynced.

What you give up: the counts are no longer visible at a glance. D135 put them on the chips ("each chip counts its own notes"); here you see only the count of the set you are in. Switching from Needs Work to Done is two taps instead of one.

C · Keep the chips, cut the rest

Four chips and nothing else to filter with. Sort moves into the search field as a word (Newest ↓) that steps on tap. The date range and Unsynced only are removed on both devices.

What you give up: the date range and Unsynced only. You kept both on 2026-07-23 when Photos and Place were cut ("Sort + Unsynced + Date on both"). Finding notes by date would then rely on scrolling the day groups.
