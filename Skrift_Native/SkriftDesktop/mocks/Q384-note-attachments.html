A video and a file inside a note, next to Apple Notes

An open Skrift note holding all four kinds of block: the new video, the new file card, and the existing photo and PDF. Phone and Mac sit next to Apple Notes redrawn by hand from Tuur’s screenshots of 13 Oct, at the same point scale (1 px here = 1 pt there). The iPad has no screenshot, so it is drawn from those two plus the signed Q378 layout. Try it: tap the video, tap a file card, press and hold any block, tap + (phone) or the paperclip (iPad, Mac).

Picked for you. Overrule any of these (the orange numbers mark them in the frames).

  1. 1Tapping the video plays it inside the note, with a small control strip and a ⤢ button for full screen. Starting the video pauses the note’s own recording, so only one thing makes sound. Apple’s screenshots do not show what its tap does.
  2. 2A tall phone video is cut to 420 pt high in the note (the poster is cropped to the middle) so the text around it stays on screen. Apple shows the full 9:16 frame, which fills the whole phone. The “Tall video” switch in the bar shows Apple’s way.
  3. 3Each kind of attachment has one size; there is no small/large switch. Video, photo and PDF are always big; any other file is always the card. Apple has a per-attachment chevron and “Set All to Small / Large” in its ⋯ menu; Skrift leaves both out.
  4. 4The file card is copied from Apple’s small card: grey card, name on up to two lines, kind · size, file icon on the right. The whole card opens Quick Look. Skrift’s old capture card with its purple “Open” button is replaced.
  5. 5The video has a duration label (bottom right, the same style as the PDF’s “38 pages”). Apple shows only the play button.
  6. 6Press and hold works like Apple: the block lifts and a menu appears (Copy, Share, Save, Delete). Move your finger without letting go and the menu closes and the block moves (D112). On the Mac you drag a block straight away and right-click for the menu.
  7. 7The PDF stays as Skrift draws it today: the first page with 14 pt corners, the “N pages” label and the “Text from the PDF” row (D126). Apple’s grey band with the filename and the page-thumbnail strip is not copied.
  8. 8Media is at most 720 pt wide on iPad and Mac (Apple’s Mac video is 720 pt), left-aligned, while the text runs the full width of the pane (D198). The PDF page is 520 pt wide there.
