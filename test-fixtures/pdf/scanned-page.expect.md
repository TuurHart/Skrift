## Letter to the harbour office

Dear harbour office, we write on behalf of the north quay survey to ask whether the council crew could look at the east steps before the autumn storms. Two railing posts worked loose in January and are held only by rope ties.

The survey has kept a daily log of the water level at the quay for three years and would be glad to share it with your office. The readings agree with the harbour gauge to within a few centimetres and may help when planning the repairs.

With thanks from the volunteers of the north quay survey.
