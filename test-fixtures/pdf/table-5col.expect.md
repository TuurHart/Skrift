## Site costs for the season

The table below lists each survey site with its visits, the volunteer hours it took, what it cost to keep running, and a short note on its state at the end of the season.

| Site | Visits | Hours | Cost (EUR) | Notes |
| North quay | 412 | 690 | 1,840 | Ladder sound |
| East steps | 158 | 240 | 2,310 | Railing loose |
| Fish market | 96 | 130 | 420 | Board faded |
| Old slipway | 71 | 115 | 380 | Logbook wet |
| South mole | 203 | 305 | 960 | Light repaired |
| Total | 940 | 1,480 | 5,910 | Within budget |

The east steps cost the most for the fewest visits because of the railing repairs, and should fall back into line once the council replaces the posts. Every other site came in under its share of the allowance.
