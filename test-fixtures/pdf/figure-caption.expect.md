## Visits through the year

Visits follow the light more than the weather. The count climbs from February as the evenings lengthen, holds through the summer, and falls away quickly once the clocks change in October. The dip in August is the missed weekend described in the rota section, not a quiet month.

[figure: Figure 1. Monthly visits to the north pier in 2025, counted from the volunteer logbook.]

The winter months are thin but never empty. A handful of regular walkers come down to the pier whatever the conditions, and their visits alone keep the logbook from going a week without an entry. Any plan to close the pier for repairs should avoid the long summer weekends and aim for the first weeks of the year instead.
