Okay so the thing I keep circling back to with the workshop is that we are trying to run two businesses out of one room and pretending it is one business. The bikes pay the rent and the ceramics pay for nothing yet, but the ceramics are the reason I get up in the morning, and Lotte says that is fine as long as I admit it out loud, which I am now doing, into a note, so it counts. The numbers from August are not terrible. Forty-one repairs, nine of them full overhauls, and the overhauls are where the margin is. If I could get that to fifteen overhauls a month I could drop the walk-in tune-ups entirely, which are the ones that eat a whole afternoon for thirty euros and a complaint about the price of brake pads. Hendrik came by on Tuesday and looked at the kiln for about a minute and said the same thing he said in March, which is that the wiring in the back room was never meant for it and that I should get Marco to look at it before something melts. He is right. I have been not calling Marco for six months because I know what Marco is going to say and what it is going to cost. The other thing is the website. Rose sent over the mockups and they are genuinely good, better than I expected, but they show the studio as a ceramics studio with a bike repair on the side, and that is the opposite of where the money comes from. I do not know if that is a problem or if that is the point. Maybe the website should show the thing I want to be and the door should show the thing I am. Jack thinks I should just raise the prices on everything by fifteen percent and see who leaves. Jack also thinks the euro is a scam and that we should be invoicing in something else, so I take his business advice with a certain amount of salt, but on the prices he might be right. Nobody has left over a price yet. People leave when the bike is not ready on the day I said.  Which brings me to scheduling, which is the actual problem. I write the promised day on the tag and then I forget the tag exists. Lotte wants me to use the calendar and I keep saying I will and I keep not doing it. If this app ends up being the place where I say things out loud then maybe it should also be the place where the tags live, I do not know, that is a different note. Mariam asked whether we would do a Saturday workshop in October, hand-building, six people, two hours. I said yes before I thought about it and now I have to think about it. Six people means six wheels or six slabs, clay for that is not the problem, the problem is that the bike side of the room has to be clean enough that nobody leans on a chain.
