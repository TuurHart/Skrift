Overhaul checklist, in order

1. Strip the drivetrain, degrease, photograph the wear.
2. Bearings: headset, bottom bracket, both hubs.
3. Cables and housing, full length, no reuse.
4. Brakes — pads and alignment.
5. Wheels true, spoke tension logged.
6. Test ride, then the tag gets the DATE.
