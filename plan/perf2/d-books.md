# Speed sweep: BOOKS (phone/iPad), 2026-10-05

Top 3 by severity:
1. D-B1: opening the player (and the quote-capture screen) full-decodes the multi-MB transcript and alignment sidecars on the main actor, several times over, then runs an O(n^2) sentence builder on the whole file.
2. D-B2: every Books-tab row does a corpus-wide note count (`noteCounts` recomputed per row over ~2,000 memos), a SwiftData fetch (`isSynced`) and per-file `fileExists`; the whole list re-renders every 5 s of playback and on every transfer-progress tick.
3. D-B3: book covers are stored and cached at full embedded resolution (no downsample), so 30 rows decode up to 30 multi-megapixel bitmaps.

Method: static read only. Nothing below is measured. "Suspect" = I did not confirm the whole call chain.
