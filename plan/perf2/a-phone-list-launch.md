Top 3 by severity:
1. A1: every Notes-list redraw re-runs a full-library pass (canonicalRows x6-7, backlink scan over every transcript, enhancement dictionary), and it redraws on every note save, every sync batch and every audiobook-transfer tick.

3. A5: `AssetMaterializer` stats about 2-5 files per note (thousands of syscalls) on the main actor on launch, first foreground, and each sync burst.

## 3. Things I checked and found fine

- The audiobook mini-player tick no longer re-renders the list (`NotesBottomChrome` owns the observation, `NotesBottomChrome.swift:10-26`).
- `MemoDate.group`/`label` use static `DateFormatter`s (`Shared/Model/MemoDate.swift:60-75`); only a `Calendar.current` lookup per memo remains.
- `allTags()` is cached on `memoSetVersion` (`NotesRepository.swift:178-184`).
- `LaunchWorkGate` itself is cheap (`fetchCount` + a one-row fetch with `propertiesToFetch`).
- CloudSyncMonitor's isSyncing hide and the import sweep are debounced to 1 s.
- `CaptureInboxDrainer.drain` is cheap when the inbox is empty.
- `MemoDeduper.run` is one fetch + a grouping; no mutation unless duplicates exist.
