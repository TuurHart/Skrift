# Reader I: images, memory, energy (both apps)

Top 3 by severity:
1. I1 Mac karaoke: while a note plays, the Mac editor decodes the full word-timing JSON, re-splits the whole body, and re-walks all text 20x a second (`NoteBody.swift:195-197`, `BodyTextView.swift:198,583`).
2. I2 Mac cloud sweep: `adoptLateDiarization` faults every non-diarized memo's audio/photo blobs on every reconcile (`MemoCloudIngest.swift:135`); prior-sweep row 8, still open.
3. I3 Phone: the whole recording is resampled into one `[Float]` array for custom-vocab boosting and for diarization (230 MB per hour of audio) (`VocabularyBooster.swift:87`, `DiarizationService.swift:50`).

Method: static read only, nothing measured. Every row names the call chain I read. Rows marked "suspect" were not fully traced.

## C. Energy and background: checked, nothing to fix

- Location: one-shot only (`Shared/Metadata/LocationOneShot.swift:26`, `requestLocation`); no continuous updates anywhere.
- Timers: phone `AudioPlayerModel` 20 Hz (`:164`) publishes only to the separate `PlayerClock` and the karaoke painter repaints only on word change (`NoteBodyView.swift:~440-470`), so the phone does not have I1. Recording timers are 4 Hz (`LiveRecordingService.swift:1622`) and the caption poll is thermal-aware (`:1608-1610`). `RecordingActivityManager` keep-alive is 60 s and only while recording.
- Audiobook: time observer 0.5 s (`AudiobookSession.swift:407`); background transcription requires external power (`BookBackgroundScheduler.swift:38`) and pauses on low battery (`BookTranscriptionJob.swift:~470-495`).
- One small leak of wakeups: `ReadAlongView.swift:175,207` runs a 0.1 s `Timer.publish` for as long as the view is on screen, including while paused (it does a linear `firstIndex` scan of the file's sentences each tick, `:117`). Low. Pause the timer when `!session.isPlaying && model.covered`.
- `Task.sleep` / `asyncAfter` use: all are debounces, toast dismissals, bounded retries or single idle-unload sleepers (`GemmaEmbedder.swift:140-150` is one sleeper that re-reads `lastUse`). No polling loop found beyond `BookTranscriptionJob.awaitRunnable` (300 ms while paused, `:392-395`; low) and the 1.5 s nudge re-check in `ReadAlongView`.
- `UserDefaults.didChangeNotification` observers exist only inside Settings sections (`ObsidianSettingsSection.swift:82,138`, `SettingsView.swift:181`); they fire on any defaults write while Settings is open. Low.

## D. Not found / confirmed fine

- `MemoImageLoader` and `PDFThumbnailLoader` do use ImageIO downsampling / sized PDF thumbnails and cache by path+mtime+size. The cache design is sound; the issues are main-thread use (I6, I7) and the stat per call.
- Mac inline photos use an ImageIO thumbnail off-main (`BodyTextView.swift:1220-1230`).
- Names/linked photos: no People photos or avatars are decoded anywhere (checked `grep` for `NSImage`/`UIImage` across both apps).
